I'm a software engineer currently based in Stockholm, Sweden 🇸🇪. I have spent the past few years at early stage startups / scale ups as a Member of Technical Staff. I like staying close to hands-on implementation in all my roles even when I have had teams or product areas to lead—in environments that can otherwise be filled with chaos, having deep knowledge and clarity about ground-level execution helps me make calmer and more prudent decisions.

I was born in India 🇮🇳 and grew up in Bangalore, a city dubbed the silicon valley of India. Growing up, I was fascinated by technology, electronics and video games. This fascination compelled me to try and build my own games. It's safe to say that I never built a video game—at least not a good one—but the experience got me hooked to the instant feedback loop of writing a line of code and knowing if it worked or not. This addiction stuck with me through my Electrical Engineering bachelors and is still the fuel that powers late night coding adventures today.

Since mid 2024, I've been living and reflecting on what it means to be a software engineer with the prevalence of AI agents. It is no longer valuable to only produce code while also not valuable to produce none—the sweet spot is somewhere in between and nobody knows where. Today, I work at Lovable, where I'm trying to figure it out; on our mission to also help the world figure it out. What a time to be alive!
